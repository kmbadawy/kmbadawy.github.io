Notes from a computational condensed matter PhD, mostly on the practical side
of things: DFT+U with spin-orbit coupling, Wannier function construction,
topological invariant workflows (Z2, Chern, nodal lines), and the scripts
that make those pipelines less painful.

Main stack: VASP, Wannier90, WannierBerri, WannierTools, z2pack, tbmodels,
irrep, spglib.
